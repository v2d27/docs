A high-level tour of the major services, how they talk to each other, and the
key trade-offs behind the current design.

## Services

The system is split into a handful of independently deployable services,
each owning a single, well-defined piece of the domain.

## Data flow

Requests enter through a single edge layer, get routed to the owning
service, and results are composed back at the edge before returning to
the client.
